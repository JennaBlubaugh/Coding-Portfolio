My UCF Journey

Why UCF?

My main pull towards UCF was because my sister had attended this college, and I wanted to gain my independence yet have a family member close. The other colleges I had applied to were Florida Atlantic University, University of Florida, and Florida State University. I got accepted to FGCU and FSU, but FSU was too far for me.

Freshmen Year

Summer

I was accepted into the summmer in 2022 and was sharing a room with a friend from high school. Little did I know that she would never leave the room at all, and didn't have any in-person classes. We are no longer friends.

Fall

I had a random roommate, but she was very nice and was a breath of fresh air. I was still adjusting to college life, and was doing my GEDs for my major, which was a pending Digital Media major. I started off as undeclared, but declared it once I was told I would be put into a course.

Spring

Finished up my main GEDs, and this would be the final semester where I would be sharing a bedroom with someone.

Sophomore Year

Summer

Finally began taking digital media classes which pertained to my major, and stayed home for the summer since I didn't have a place to live and preferred going home.

Fall

I begin taking an art class and finish up a semester in my Spanish class. In the art class I met a very nice group of people and found people who were also in my major. My Spanish was improving, and by the end of this semester, I would be able to declare as a Web Design major officially.

Spring

This is the current semester, and I am taking Client-side Scripting, Front end web design, and digital cultures and naratives. I am also taking one additional art class as an extra. After getting hands-on experience coding, I believe I have picked the best major for me, as it is equally challenging and rewarding.
